  • Issue, create, or mint new QZ outside the protocol's consensus rules

  • Access users' private keys or funds

4. Not a Security

Quartz was designed and is marketed as a decentralized payment network and IoT economy tool — not as an investment contract. Key facts:

  • No initial coin offering (ICO), token sale, or fundraising event

6. Dev Fund Transparency

The Quartz dev fund (5% of total supply, 2,100,000 QZ) is governed as follows:

  • Emission: 2.5 QZ per block, emitted automatically by protocol consensus rules
  • Vesting: ~4 years (~525,600 blocks), ending after block 525,600
  • Governance: Multi-signature control (target: 3-of-5), not controlled by any single individual
  • Purpose: Development, security audits, bug bounty (50,000 QZ), and ecosystem grants
  • Public address: Published on-chain and in the block explorer
  • Spending log: All dev fund transactions are visible on the public blockchain

8. Hardware Safety

ESP32 devices are electrical hardware. Follow manufacturer safety guidelines:

  • Use appropriate power supplies (USB 5V, or battery per board spec)
  • Do not expose to water unless properly enclosed (IP65+ for outdoor use)
  • LoRa antennas must comply with regional frequency regulations (EU 868, US 915, etc.) and power limits
  • Solar installations should follow local electrical codes
  • Flash encryption and secure boot, once enabled, are irreversible — understand the implications before provisioning
