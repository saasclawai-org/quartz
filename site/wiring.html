🔮 Quartz — M5Stack Core Wiring Guide

Connect a relay module for QR-paymented physical control. $15 ESP32 + $2 relay = crypto vending machine.

Wiring Connections

M5Stack Pin Relay Pin Wire Color Purpose
GPIO26 SIG / IN 🟢 Green Relay control signal (3.3V logic)
3.3V VCC 🔴 Red Relay power (some relays need 5V — use 5V/VIN pin instead)
GND GND ⚫ Black Common ground
— COM 🟡 Yellow Common terminal → Load power in
— NO 🟡 Yellow Normally Open → Load positive (closes on payment)

Payment Flow

  1. Display — M5Stack shows QR code with quartz:address?amount=0.50
  2. Scan — Customer scans QR with phone wallet app
  3. Pay — Phone sends QZ to device's wallet address via node
  4. Verify — ESP32 polls node every 5s, detects incoming payment
  5. Trigger — Payment confirmed → GPIO26 goes HIGH → relay closes → load activates
⚠️ Relay Power Note: Most relay modules need 5V on VCC, not 3.3V. The M5Stack Core has a 5V pin on the bottom expansion port (labeled 5V). If your relay doesn't click with 3.3V, move the VCC wire to the 5V pin. The GPIO26 signal is 3.3V logic — this is fine for triggering most relay modules.
💡 Tip: For a vending machine, wire the relay between the dispensing motor's power and the PSU's NO (Normally Open) terminal. When payment confirms, the relay closes for 3 seconds — just enough to dispense one item.
