Radio Discipline

The Meshtastic community guards its airtime — a 230-byte packet at long-range settings costs seconds of it. We agree, and we designed around it.

🔇

Own Channel, Own Sync Word

Quartz firmware never joins, hears, or transmits on Meshtastic channels. We use our own LoRa parameters when radio transport ships — your mesh and ours coexist without collision. Zero shared airtime, zero grievance.

📉

Sparsity-First Protocol

Off-grid mining is designed to be nearly silent: a compact work beacon every ~10 minutes, mining itself completely silent, and a single packet transmitted only when a block is won. That's ~6–12 packets per hour for an entire swarm — comfortably inside EU 1% duty-cycle rules.

🤝

Hardware, Not Airtime

Meshtastic taught the world that mesh works without the internet — we're not going to repay that by flooding it. Quartz rides the boards, never the network. Flash back anytime; your node is still a node.

How Off-Grid Mining Works

Battery board. No WiFi, no node, no internet. It still earns.

1️⃣

Catch a Work Beacon

A gateway broadcasts the current block height and target — one tiny packet about every 10 minutes. Every board in radio range picks it up.

2️⃣

Mine in Silence

Each board grinds SHA-256 proof-of-work on-device at ~1.2W. Wallet keys (quantum-safe WOTS+) never leave the chip. Radio stays off.

3️⃣

Transmit on a Win

A solved block announces itself in a single packet. Any internet-connected board relays it to the chain — and the reward lands on the miner that found it. Gateway-relay economics ship with the Relayer Pool fork (roadmap).

Today this loop runs in production on the ESP-NOW mesh (~200m, WiFi band) — work distribution and block relay are live on the testnet. LoRa transport extends the same pattern to kilometer range and is on the roadmap.
